Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026 · Applies to FRIDAY 0.0.25 and later until replaced

FRIDAY is made by Nandivex Technologies ("we"). This page says in plain words what the app reads, where it goes, and what you can do about it. Questions: contact@nandivex.in.

The short version

  • FRIDAY needs Android's accessibility permission to see your screen and tap and type for you. It does this only while you use it by voice.
  • What you say and the screen content FRIDAY reads are sent to Google Gemini to work out what to do. You use your own Gemini key, so this goes from your phone to Google, not through our servers.
  • Your memories, history and settings are kept on your phone.
  • We use Google Firebase for crash reports.
  • We do not sell your data and we show no ads.

What FRIDAY reads and sends

Your voice

While a session is open, the microphone audio is streamed to the Gemini Live service so it can understand you and reply. FRIDAY does not record audio to a file.

Your screen

When you ask FRIDAY to do something on the phone, it reads the text and buttons that are on the screen. If an app shows no readable text, it can send a screenshot instead. This can include private things that are on the screen at that moment, such as messages, names, numbers, one-time codes or amounts. Do not ask FRIDAY to look at a screen you do not want Google to see.

Other phone information

If you ask for it, FRIDAY reads and may send things like the battery level, time, the Wi-Fi name, which Bluetooth devices are paired, the text on your clipboard, and the list of apps that can be opened.

Your Gemini key and Google

You add your own Gemini API key. It is stored encrypted on your phone and is sent to Google only to authorise your requests. What Google does with the data you send is covered by Google's Gemini API terms and the terms of the plan your key belongs to. For example, Google may use content sent with a free key to improve its products. Check which plan your key is on.

What stays on your phone

  • Memory: short notes about you that FRIDAY saves when you share a lasting fact. You can read, edit and delete them on the Memory page.
  • History: your past conversations, shown on the History page. You can delete one, a day, or all of it.
  • Settings: language, voice, reply style and your name.

Uninstalling FRIDAY, or clearing its data in Android's settings, removes all of this.

What we collect through Firebase

FRIDAY uses Google Firebase for crash reports only. It gives us, and stores on Google's servers:

  • Crash reports (Crashlytics): what failed, the app version, your phone model and Android version.

We use this only to keep FRIDAY working and to fix problems. See Firebase's privacy information for how Google handles it. We will update this page, and ask you where the law requires it, before FRIDAY starts using accounts or payments.

Permissions, and why

Accessibility
See the screen and tap, type, scroll and swipe for you. Off until you turn it on.
Microphone
Hear you during a session.
Location
Android shows the Wi-Fi name only to apps with location access. FRIDAY does not track your location and never asks for background location.
Nearby devices
Show and switch Bluetooth.
Notifications
Show the "FRIDAY is listening" notice.
Do Not Disturb, system settings
Change Do Not Disturb, brightness, screen timeout and rotation when you ask.

Your choices

  • Turn accessibility off in Android's settings at any time. FRIDAY stops being able to see or touch the screen.
  • Delete your memories and history in the app, or uninstall it.
  • Ask us to delete the Firebase data linked to your app instance by writing to contact@nandivex.in. Include the version shown in About.

Children

FRIDAY is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will change the date above and tell you in the app.
