Privacy Terms

Your phone, on voice command.

FRIDAY is a hands-free assistant for Android. Say what you want done and it operates the phone: it reads the screen, taps, types, scrolls, opens apps and switches settings, so you do not have to touch it.

Not available yet. FRIDAY is being prepared for Google Play and cannot be downloaded from this site. It needs Android 8 or newer.

What it can do

See and use the screen

Reads what is on screen, then taps, types, scrolls and swipes like a finger would.

Move around the phone

Back, home, recent apps, notification shade, quick settings, lock screen and screenshots.

Switch settings

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data panel, airplane mode, hotspot, NFC, dark mode, battery saver.

Sound, display and torch

Volume, ringer, Do Not Disturb, brightness, screen timeout, flashlight and SOS blink, music keys.

Need something else, such as a call, a message or an alarm? Ask, and FRIDAY opens the app and uses it for you, the way your own hands would.

Built to be clear about what it does

Phone control uses Android's accessibility permission. FRIDAY asks for it when you first open the app and explains why. While you use it, text from your screen is sent to Google Gemini to carry out what you ask. You can turn the permission off at any time in Android's settings.
  • You bring your own Gemini key, so your conversations go straight from your phone to Google, not through our servers.
  • Your memories and history stay on your phone.
  • FRIDAY is a general hands-free assistant. It is not a medical or emergency device.
